~ 3 Golden Rules

1Always include a number
"Cut UI dev time 40%" beats "improved performance". Numbers are proof.
2End open
Always leave a hook - a question, a goal, an invitation - so they have a reason to continue.
3Don't memorize word-for-word
Know the structure + your key numbers. Deliver naturally. The script is a map, not a script.
